Why Most Marketing Proposals Get Ghosted

Sending a cold list of deliverables without a strategic decision framework kills your deals in three distinct ways:

1. You Become a Commodity

When clients only see a list of tasks, they immediately compare your price against cheaper competitors on Upwork or Fiverr.

2. Unguided Choice Paralysis

Without clear 3-tier options, prospects feel overwhelmed trying to guess what they actually need—so they make no decision at all.

3. The Email Inbox Abyss

Sending a proposal without a pre-planned follow-up sequence means your offer gets buried under daily unread messages.
